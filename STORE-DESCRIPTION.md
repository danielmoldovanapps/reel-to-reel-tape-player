# Reel-to-Reel Tape Player 1.3.0

A vintage-inspired reel-to-reel music player for Linux, supporting music from your computer and network locations. Enjoy animated tape reels, responsive VU meters, album artwork, a persistent playlist, and customizable deck and reel branding. You can also brand the interface to your liking. If you like the app, please consider donating to the developer.
